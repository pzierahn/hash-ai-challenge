## About the role

As a senior **Product Designer** working on **Novel AI & World Modeling Interfaces** you’ll be the steward of our end-to-end product experience: establishing a robust Figma design system, crafting high-fidelity prototypes and production-ready designs, and partnering with engineers to ship pixel-perfect features. You’ll join a cross-functional squad of UX researchers, UI designers, AI scientists, frontend developers, and product managers pushing the limits of what’s possible in the browser, building systems to deliver “Safeguarded AI”.

## What you'll work on

- Own our Figma design system: produce scalable components, typographic styles, tokens, and documentation for use across multiple high-calibre internal and external teams
- Translate user stories into journey maps, wireframes, and polished UI ready for user testing cycles, and later production
- Develop intuitive, informative interfaces which enable non-technical users to create models of real world processes, providing abstractions and assistance on top of the complexity of the underlying theory
- Build interactive prototypes (Figma/Web) to validate flows and micro-interactions with test users
- Facilitate design critiques and collaborate closely with engineering to ensure design intent is faithfully realized
- Champion accessibility and inclusive design across typography, color, motion, and copy

## Requirements

### Must have

- Demonstrable experience designing complex web apps with a modern design-system mindset
- Advanced mastery of Figma: beyond ordinary component libraries, variants, tokens, shared styles, and auto-layout
- Strong visual craft (typography, colour, grid) paired with systematic thinking
- Comfort prototyping at multiple fidelities and presenting rationale to technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Solid understanding of WCAG 2.2 and pragmatic accessibility techniques
- Excellent written & verbal English; thrive in async, documentation-heavy culture

### Nice to have

- Experience designing data-dense products (simulations, modeling, analytics), AI-user interactions, or user-built views and models (e.g. process flows, dashboards).
- Hands-on UX research: facilitating questionnaires/workshops, planning studies, analysing insights, turning findings into action
- Interest in real-time collaboration (i.e. multiplayer editing) and how it shapes UX patterns
